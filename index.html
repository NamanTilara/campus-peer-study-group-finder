How it works

From searching to your first session takes a few minutes.

  1. Search your subject

    Enter a course or pick a subject area to see circles with open seats.

  2. Join a circle

    Check the time and place, then request a seat. The host confirms it.

  3. Study and swap notes

    Meet, solve past papers together and explain topics to each other.
